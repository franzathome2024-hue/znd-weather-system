    Sources, boundaries, disclaimers

    Forecasts are representative point values at each municipal centre; inland and upland barangays will run cooler, coastal barangays may see different highs. The consensus is a robust weighted mean (PAGASA 2.5, ECMWF/UKMO/NCEP-AIGFS 1.5, other NWP centres 1.0, AIFS 0.5) with outlier values over 2.5σ from the median trimmed before weighting, falling back to a median when fewer than three independent values agree. Contributor counts are reported separately for high and low.

    The municipal overlay is a generated Voronoi approximation, not an official PSA/PSGC boundary, used for visualization only. Basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors. Always follow DOST-PAGASA tropical cyclone warnings and rainfall advisories for official decisions.
